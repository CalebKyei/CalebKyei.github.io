Selected projects

  • CITCEA-UPC, Barcelona · since Sep 2026 · individual In progress

    Grid-connected converter control

    An averaged grid-following voltage-source converter with an SRF-PLL and decoupled d-q current control. Swept short-circuit ratio and X/R to assess stability against PLL bandwidth, and added DC-link voltage control, current limiting and anti-windup.

    Grid-forming control of a modular multilevel converter (MMC) is a planned extension, to be completed before the thesis.

    • Simscape
    • SRF-PLL
    • d-q control
    • Weak grid

  • HTW Berlin · 2026 · team project

    EV powertrain with model predictive control

    A Simulink model of a 1.55 MW quad-motor EV drive with PMSM field-oriented control and adaptive cruise control (ISO 15622 constant time gap). My part: the state-space MPC speed controller. Speed overshoot stayed below 0.05 km/h.

    • Simulink
    • MPC
    • PMSM FOC

  • HTW Berlin · 2026

    Day-ahead solar forecasting

    A Python day-ahead solar forecast for the 50Hertz area, built from public ENTSO-E data: a physics-based PV ensemble with spread-adaptive quantiles. RMSE under 6% of the 11.6 GW mean daily peak, ranking 2nd of 16 entries, behind only the TSO baseline.

    • Python
    • Probabilistic forecasting
    • ENTSO-E

  • École Centrale de Nantes · 2025 – 2026

    2 MW DFIG wind turbine supervisory control

    A supervisory finite-state machine that switches between MPPT and rated-power modes with anti-chattering hysteresis, coordinating rotor-side converter torque with the pitch actuators. Stability and Cp optimisation validated in MATLAB/Simulink under turbulent wind.

    • MATLAB/Simulink
    • Supervisory control
    • Wind
