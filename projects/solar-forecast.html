Day-ahead solar forecasting

A day-ahead forecast of solar generation in the 50Hertz area, built in Python from public data and scored by RMSE against the other entries and the TSO baseline.

Area
50Hertz control area (ENTSO-E data)
Data
Public data only
Method
Physics-based PV ensemble (tilt and azimuth calibration) with spread-adaptive quantile selection
Type
Individual entry in a course forecasting assignment
Tools
Python
When
2026
Entries ranked by RMSE; only the TSO baseline scored better
2nd / 16
RMSE of the forecast
690.08 MW
RMSE of the TSO baseline, the one entry ahead
567.09 MW
RMSE as a share of the 11.6 GW mean daily peak
< 6 %

Results

  • Ranking. 2nd of 16 entries on RMSE, behind only the TSO baseline at 567.09 MW. This forecast's RMSE was about 22 % above the baseline's: the baseline's point forecast was the more accurate.
  • Scale. An RMSE of 690.08 MW is under 6 % of the 11.6 GW mean daily peak of solar generation in the area.
